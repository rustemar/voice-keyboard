Voice Keyboard is a keyboard that types what you say. Switch to it in any text field, tap the microphone, speak, and the text lands in whatever app you are using.

It has no speech engine of its own and no account. Bring an API key for a supported speech service, such as Groq or Mistral, or run a server of your own. Audio goes only to the endpoint you configure. Compatible Chat Completions services can also transcribe audio; the selected model must accept your device's recording format.

<b>Voice input</b>
<ul><li>Works in any text field, in any app</li><li>Processing queue: start the next recording while the previous one is still being transcribed</li><li>Cancel only the latest unfinished dictation during recording, recognition, post-processing or retry; earlier recordings continue</li><li>Optional voice input for other apps through Android speech recognition or a dictation window. Off by default, uses your saved provider, without post-processing or context</li><li>Offline-proof: a recording that cannot be sent is kept on the device and resent automatically once the connection returns</li><li>Several dictation languages on one key: tap to cycle, long-press to pick from the list</li><li>Custom vocabulary for names and technical terms that get misrecognised</li><li>Can be the voice input of another keyboard, such as HeliBoard, and hand back to it once the text is typed</li></ul>

<b>Optional post-processing</b>
<ul><li>Fix punctuation, spelling and filler words</li><li>Shorten, add emoji, rewrite as verse, or translate</li><li>Optional recent dictation context helps recover misheard names and terms. Off by default; remembers up to 5 of your own recent dictations only in memory and sends them to the selected provider as reference. Context is shared across chats in the same app and clears when input moves to another app. It does not read conversations.</li><li>Terminal mode turns dictation into shell commands</li><li>Works with OpenAI, Anthropic, or any OpenAI-compatible provider such as OpenRouter; every prompt is editable</li></ul>

<b>Keyboard</b>
<ul><li>One tap takes you back to the keyboard you came from</li><li>Optional compact Terminal panel with full-size controls and instant expansion; off by default</li><li>Punctuation keys, accelerating backspace, send key, persistent clipboard bar</li><li>The Enter key runs the field's own action: Search, Go, Send, Next or Done</li><li>The microphone is off in password fields</li><li>Spacing around dictated text follows the text already in the field</li><li>Light, dark and automatic themes; 17 interface languages</li></ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
<ul><li>No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account, no servers of our own</li><li>API keys and settings stay on the device and are excluded from cloud backup</li><li>Audio is sent only to the endpoint you configured, authenticated with your own key</li></ul>

The app can also look for new versions on GitHub: automatically only if you switch that on, or when you press "Check for updates". Updates obtained that way come straight from the developer and are not reviewed by F-Droid.
